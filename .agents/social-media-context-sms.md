## Target Audience

- **Primary audience**: English-speaking 18–35 year olds who like travel, self-improvement and "psychology facts" content
- **Pain points**: Burnout, rushing, comparing themselves, feeling stuck, scrolling instead of living
- **Goals**: Calm, perspective, small motivation, travel inspiration, something interesting to share
- **Sophistication level**: Beginner–intermediate (pop psychology level, no jargon)
- **Where they hang out**: TikTok (For You feed); later cross-post to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts

---

## Voice & Tone

- **Voice adjectives**: calm, warm, reflective, concise, curious
- **Formality**: Casual
- **Humor**: None in reminders/proverbs; occasional and playful in Weird facts
- **Phrases to use**: Short two-beat lines (setup… / …payoff), plain words, second person ("you")
- **Phrases to avoid**: "rise and grind", "sigma", "alpha", hustle-culture tone, clickbait like "you won't believe"

---

## Content Pillars

1. **Proverbs × Psychology** — a proverb from one country (original language + English), then the real psychological idea behind it in one line. Flagship series.
2. **Daily reminder** — calm vlog footage with one short motivation or psychology message (two beats, under 12 words each).
3. **Weird facts** — funny or surprising facts, faster cuts, bigger text. Every fact must be verifiable; link the source in the caption or a comment.
4. **Vlog** — moments from the day told through short on-screen captions.

- **Topics to avoid**: Politics, religion debates, medical/mental-health advice presented as diagnosis

- **Content types to avoid**: Made-up statistics, fake or misattributed quotes/proverbs, unverified "facts"
